Privacy Policy

Effective: November 18, 2026 · Pilot edition (1st edition)

We are in a pilot period. We will announce changes in the app first.

AP Golf Co., Ltd. (the "Company") operates AP Lesson Note (the "Service") and complies with the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea and other applicable laws. This policy explains what information we collect and why, where and how long we keep it, and who can see it.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email and login identifier received through Google sign-in; the in-app name (if changed) and crew tone setting.
  • Age group, country and consent records: the age group chosen at first sign-in (under 14 / 14–18 / 19 and over), country of residence, and the time and version of consent (for China, including the separate consent to cross-border transfer and sensitive information).
  • Guardian information (only for ages that require guardian consent, see section 7): guardian's name, relationship and contact number, and the result of the coach's guardian check.
  • My Statement: basic bio (years playing, age, gender, handicap, practice volume, play frequency) and self-ratings and measurements in six areas (mental, short game, shots, physical, course management, club fitting).
  • Special part · Yips record (optional): status, which shots, symptoms, when, how often and how severe. Because this may include physical symptoms, it is recorded only if you choose to.
  • Lesson records: the coach name you enter, lesson date, the coach's words as typed or dictated (kept as the original), the AI-organized result (problems, cause hypotheses, drills, etc.), and the language of the original and of the result.
  • My problem statements: the shots, ball flights and frequency you select, any words you add, and the AI-generated problem, solution, drills and plan.
  • Training records: mandalart, battle plan and check-offs, training cards (proposal, adjustments, actual runs, reviews), feedback.
  • Crew conversations: questions and answers in "Ask the crew".
  • Language settings: app language and the speaking language used for dictation (stored on your device only).
  • Connection information: connection links you create, the name and role of people who request access, approval status and time.
  • Usage counts: how many times per day you used AI features (to enforce daily limits).

Language identification: when organizing a lesson record or statement, the AI identifies the language of the original (for example, a coach who spoke English) and stores it with the result, which is shown in the language you selected. The original text is kept unchanged.

Voice input: the microphone button uses your browser's dictation feature. The Company does not receive or store the audio, only the transcribed text. Dictation may be processed by the browser provider (for example, Google for Google Chrome).

1-2. Information about other people that you enter

Lesson records include the coach's name and what the coach said, as entered by you. Please let your coach know before you record. Coaches may request access to, correction of, or deletion of records about them using the contact in section 9.

2. Why we use it

  • Organizing lesson records; creating and showing solutions, drills, mandalarts and battle plans from problem statements
  • Crew conversations and feedback (answers are based on your own records and cite them)
  • Sharing records with coaches, parents and others you have approved
  • Growing the shared Legacy Tree (section 4)
  • Guardian checks, daily usage limits and preventing misuse
  • Improving the Service (section 6-2)

The Company does not use this information for advertising and does not sell your information.

4. The shared Legacy Tree

When you organize a lesson, the server adds the lesson points from that lesson (e.g., "hands ahead of the ball at impact") to the Legacy Tree that everyone grows together. Identical points are merged and only their sources increase.

  • Whatever language a lesson was organized in, its points are merged into one reference text (Korean) with AI translations into all six languages, so everyone sees them in their chosen language. Points with the same meaning are merged even across languages.
  • Sources appear as coach name · player label · date. Adult players are shown by family name only (e.g., "Kim○○ player"); players under 19 are shown only as "Junior player". Emails and account identifiers are never included.
  • Your own problem statements are not added to the Legacy Tree.
  • Because it is shared knowledge, points already added remain in masked form even after you delete your account. To have a specific point removed, contact us per section 9.

5. Who can see it

  • You: all of your records.
  • People you approve (connections): can only read your lesson records, problem statements, statement, mandalart and battle plan. Only you can approve, and you can disconnect at any time.
  • AP coaches and administrators: may view player records for coaching, guardian checks and record review.
  • All users: only the lesson points and masked sources in the shared Legacy Tree.

6. Service providers and international processing

The Company is located in the Republic of Korea. Information of users in other countries is also processed in Korea and in the countries of the providers below. The providers may not use information for anything beyond the work entrusted to them under contract.

The Company does not provide user records for AI model training. If you do not want international processing you may choose not to use AI features, but then you cannot receive organizing, conversations or training suggestions.

6-2. Research and service improvement

  • To refine the AP Swing Learning Matrix and training rules, we use only statistics that cannot identify individuals.
  • Only records of users who turned on Research participation in Settings are pseudonymized and used for internal research. You can use the Service the same way without it and can turn it off at any time.
  • Records of users at ages that require guardian consent (section 7) are not used for research.
  • When research results are published, only group statistics that do not reveal individuals are used.

6-3. Automatically generated results

AI and rules automatically generate problems, cause hypotheses, drills, mandalarts and battle plans. Causes are shown as hypotheses to be checked, the type of evidence (coach judgment / own statement / AI estimate) is labeled, and the coach's judgment comes first. These results are not used for decisions such as refusing sign-up or restricting use.

7. Minors

  • Ages that require guardian consent: under 14 in all countries, and up to 18 in Indonesia and Malaysia (broader than the U.S. under-13 standard).
  • Users at these ages need guardian consent and cannot use AI features until an AP coach has verified the guardian. If we learn that information was collected without guardian consent, we delete it without delay.
  • The AI only discusses golf and does not ask minors for personal information. If there is pain it advises stopping and consulting someone, and it does not give diet or medication instructions.
  • Minors appear in Legacy sources only as "Junior player".
  • We advise confirming and approving connections together with a parent or guardian. Guardians may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their child's information using the contact in section 9.

8. Your rights and our safeguards

You may request access to, correction of, deletion of, or suspension of processing of your information at any time. You can do these directly in the app: edit your statement and mandalart, disconnect connections, delete your account. To delete a single record or receive your records as a file, contact us per section 9.

All transmissions are encrypted with HTTPS, and database security rules allow only you, people you approved and AP coaches to access records. The Company does not store passwords (Google sign-in), and AI access keys and judgment rules are kept on the server only. Your device stores only screen settings such as staying signed in, language and vibration; we do not use advertising or tracking cookies. The microphone is used only when you press voice input, after your browser asks for permission.
